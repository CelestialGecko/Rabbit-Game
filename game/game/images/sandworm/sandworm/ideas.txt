At first, it mimics the shape of a crystal(sandworm idle), and when the player gets close, it activates the monster(sandworm warning & sandworm warning effect)

The alert will last for a short period of time, possibly a few seconds, giving the player a little time to react

After triggering the alarm (red circle(sandworm warning effect), crystal trembling(sandworm warning)), make the first attack(sandworm attack)

After completing the attack, they remain in a position to drill out of the ground, perhaps for a few seconds or more(sandworm idle2), to allow the player to attack

Then drill back to the ground, use movement to chase the player(sandworm move), stop right under the player, and repeat the above attack style

Enter the death animation after being killed

Convulsions(sandworm die start) for a short time followed by explosions(sandworm die finish)
